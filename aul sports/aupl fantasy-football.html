How to play

Save 15 players within your round’s credit budget: 2 goalkeepers, 5 defenders, 5 midfielders and 3 forwards. Choose 11 starters and a captain. Pick no more than 3 players from any club. The bench does not score and there are no automatic substitutions.

Save before the displayed deadline. Teams lock automatically at that time. Only official statistics from the round’s fixtures count. Points appear after the admin publishes checked results. Each round has a separate team; your previous round stays in your account. Credits are imaginary and this competition is free.
